Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about PENSA-UMaT

Who can join PENSA-UMaT? +

PENSA-UMaT is open to all students of the University of Mines and Technology, regardless of denomination. Whether you're a fresher or a final-year student, you're welcome to join our family!

What time are Sunday services? +

Sunday services are held from 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM at the UMaT Main Campus Auditorium. We also have midweek services on Tuesdays from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM, and bi-weekly Wing Meetings (Gent Wing & Lady Wing) on Mondays.

How can I join a department? +

Visit our Departments page to learn about each ministry and submit a request to join. You can also speak to any of our leaders after service.

Can I submit a prayer request? +

Absolutely! Use the contact form above and select "Prayer Request" as the subject. Our prayer team will intercede on your behalf. All prayer requests are treated with strict confidentiality.

How can I support PENSA-UMaT financially? +

Visit our Give page to make tithes, offerings, or special donations. You can give via Mobile Money, bank transfer, or card payment. Every contribution makes a difference!
